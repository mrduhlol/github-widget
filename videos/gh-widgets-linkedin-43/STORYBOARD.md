## Video direction

Palette (frame.md, hand-corrected to the GitHub-dark brand): ground ink-black #060A07 / #0B110C, text cream #E9F5EA, single accent fire-orange→contribution green #3DFF6E, muted #7F917F, hairlines #1E2C20. Two registers only — dark ground for tour beats, accent-ground reserved for the F7 end-card lockup. Type by role: Anton display (lowercase, negative tracking) for statements, IBM Plex Mono uppercase chrome for kickers/labels.

Motion grammar: one camera, one feel — smooth long-tail eases (power3 default), every reveal cued to its spoken phrase, nothing on screen before the VO names it, reveals weighted to the back ~50%. 3D camera: tilt-to-flat settles on device surfaces, slow pushes under type beats, single push-through only at section bounds (carried by the harness zoom-through into F2/F7). Holds stay alive with subtle jitter at most — no breathing, no drift, no orbit during a read.

Rhythm: F1 tail, F4 tail and F7 are held reads (stillness is the payoff); F2 is the breather prelude; F3–F6 reveal continuously to the VO. All frame seams are deliberate clean cuts carried by the harness transitions — no continuing element crosses a boundary, so no handoff blocks.

Negative list: never front-load-then-freeze (slideshow); never float everything independently (screensaver); no purple-blue AI gradients; no browser chrome, nav bars or scrollbars; never redraw the GitHub mark or the avatar — staged assets only; nothing important in the bottom ~17% caption band.
