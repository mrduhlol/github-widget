The app, screen by screen.

Small app. Careful details.

About 5.6 MB. Android 8 and up. Nothing to sign into.

  • Works offline

    The last graph stays on screen and refreshes on its own, every 1 to 12 hours.

  • Match your wallpaper

    On Android 12 and newer, the graph can take its color from your wallpaper.

  • Fits any size

    The graph fills the widget. Make it wider to see more weeks; small sizes drop text first.

  • What you see is what you get

    The in-app preview is drawn by the same code as the widget itself.

  • No account, no token

    It reads your public contribution graph. Just type your username or paste your profile link.

  • Tap to refresh

    Tap the widget to open the app and pull the latest data right away.

On your home screen in under a minute.
